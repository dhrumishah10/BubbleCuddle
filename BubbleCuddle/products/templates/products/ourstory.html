A Sudsy Start

Founded in 2023, BubbleCuddle began in a small kitchen with a simple mission: to turn the nightly bath routine into a sanctuary of comfort. We noticed that most products were either too chemical-heavy or just... boring.

We spent 18 months perfecting our **"Cloud-Touch"** formula—ensuring that every bomb, salt, and lather feels as soft as a cuddle and smells like a dream.
